# Spike

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Rakesh is the initial user, learning SNNs on an iPhone during a commute. The long-term goal is to read, reproduce, and write research on spiking neural networks.

## Product Purpose

Teach one concept at a time through a short explanation, a useful diagram, worked numbers, and retrieval questions. Help the learner distinguish lesson completion from durable understanding.

## Operating Context

Installable PWA with local progress, offline lessons, streaks, spaced reviews, prerequisite checks, feedback notes, backup export/import, and an optional copyable ChatGPT prompt. Keep existing progress compatible.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Every authored lesson gets a diagram. Add controls selectively when exploring a mechanism helps explain it. Figures and explanations are authored data and work offline. No AI API is needed for this pass. No subscriptions or third-party analytics. Any future AI integration is an open decision.

## Brand Commitments

Spike. Plain, direct language: name the idea and explain the mechanism. Avoid motivational filler and authoring notes in learner-facing text. This pass keeps the existing dark/light themes and green accent while improving the teaching surface.

## Evidence on Hand

27 authored SNN lessons, quizzes and retries, a prerequisite graph, local progress and feedback. User screenshots identify dense paragraphs, vague headings, and authoring instructions in lesson 23. Tests and browser previews do not establish physical iPhone behavior.

## Product Principles

- Diagrams should explain something the paragraph alone makes hard to see.
- Show intuition and numbers before symbols.
- Use delayed, independent retrieval as stronger evidence than immediate supported answers.
- Keep the next action reachable on a phone.
